why this way made

This ranking system was made because I wanted a more accurate ranking list. Other lists (such as brawlmance) only got recent information, and did not remember past rankings. This led to many players with high elo simply not being displayed if they hadn't played ranked in a day or so. This ranked system was made with the pure intent to remember rankings, in an attempt to be more accurate for everyone.
